# BarChart
Weekly volume bars: past weeks neutral, this week lime, planned volume dashed.

- Past weeks: `line-strong`. Current week actual: `accent`, drawn over a dashed outline of its planned km. Future weeks: dashed outline only.
- Three gridlines at most (0, half, top) in `line`; axis text in `ink-subtle`. The top label carries the unit.
- The same chart shows the plan's shape on the Plan tab (12 bars, lighter weeks visible as dips).
